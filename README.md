## ❓ Research Questions & Key Insights

### 🔍 Research Questions:

* Which customers are more likely to churn?
* Does geography impact churn?
* How do financial attributes (balance, credit score) affect churn?
* Is there a relationship between salary and churn?

### 📈 Key Insights:

* Overall churn rate: **20.37%** (~1 in 5 customers leave)
* **Germany** has the highest churn (~32%)
* Customers with **only 1 product** churn the most
* Customers aged **40–60 with high balances** are more likely to churn
* Estimated salary has **minimal impact** on churn
